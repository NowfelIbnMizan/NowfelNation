Common questions

Which affiliate platforms do you work with?

Impact, ShareASale, CJ, UpPromote, Refersion, FirstPromoter, Affise and ClickBank, among others. If you have not chosen one yet, I will recommend the best fit for your budget and model.

Do I need an existing affiliate program?

No. Setup & Launch builds one from scratch. If you already have a program, start with the Program Audit or go straight to Program Management.

Who will I actually work with?

Me, directly. I handle strategy, outreach and reporting myself, so you always know who is responsible for your program.

How soon will I see results?

New partners usually join in the first weeks of outreach. Steady affiliate revenue normally takes a few months to build, depending on your offer, commission and market.

How do reports work?

You get a biweekly or monthly report covering clicks, conversions, revenue, top partners and the next actions, plus a call to go through it.

How do I pay?

Prices are in EUR. One-off projects are invoiced before work starts; management is invoiced monthly.
